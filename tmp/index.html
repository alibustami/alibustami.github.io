$1,000 Educational Portfolio Plan

50 slices x $20 each. Built for learning, diversification across narratives, and risk awareness (not safety guarantee).

Last updated: 2026-02-28

Risk definitions: Medium (larger/liquid), High (mid-cap + execution risk), Extreme (small cap, meme, thin liquidity, or complex protocol risk).

50 Slices

Methodology

  • Screen by market-cap bands and active trading volume (liquidity proxy) from CoinGecko snapshots.
  • Review token distribution/vesting schedules from official docs and tokenomics pages before deployment.
  • Check developer activity (GitHub commits/contributors) and protocol traction (TVL/fees where relevant).
  • Require contract verification + reputable audits/bug bounty disclosures before any real deployment.

Red flags

  • Low liquidity / large spreads
  • Concentrated holders or near-term unlock cliffs
  • Unverified contracts, fake audit claims, or unresolved critical exploits
  • Wash-trading patterns and inorganic volume spikes

Risk & Safety Basics

  • Prefer hardware wallets for larger balances; split hot/cold wallets.
  • Double-check URLs and signer prompts; avoid blind approvals.
  • Revoke stale token approvals periodically.
  • Bridge only when necessary and limit size per transfer.

How to Use (Educational)

  • Optional DCA ideas: 5–10 slices per week to reduce timing concentration.
  • Rebalancing: time-based (e.g., monthly/quarterly) rather than price prediction.
  • 50 slices diversify narratives; diversification reduces concentration risk, not total risk.
